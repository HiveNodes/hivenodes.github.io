The mission, step by step

01 / 09

The operator sets one intent: search this valley. Every aircraft carries the whole plan.

  1. Intent. The operator sets one intent: search this valley. Every aircraft carries the whole plan.
  2. Share the work. The aircraft split the area between them. No leader: each takes the lanes it can reach first.
  3. Ground team. Ground robots take the shoreline road, on the same plan and the same network.
  4. Relay. Aircraft up high pass messages on for robots and boats beyond radio range.
  5. GPS jammed. Every vehicle navigates on its own sensors and checks its position against its neighbours.
  6. Aircraft lost. One goes silent. The others take over its lanes, with no one telling them to.
  7. The coast. The sweep reaches the shoreline, where boats are tasked to watch the water.
  8. Found. An aircraft spots the target. It keeps it in view while the rest hold the search.
  9. The operator decides. The find goes to the operator, who decides what happens next. Then the fleet comes home and lands, one by one.

How the system is designed to work. The aircraft steps are flown in simulation; ground robots and boats are in development. Film rendered.
